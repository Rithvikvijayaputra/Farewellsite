Four years. One journey.

Goodbyes aren't the end of our friendship — just the start of staying in touch from different desks.

After four years at Wipro, I'm stepping into what comes next. This chapter began on the ELC (Estée Lauder Companies) account, where I learned enterprise Exchange administration from the ground up, and grew into someone comfortable running Microsoft 365 and cloud messaging infrastructure at scale — PowerShell scripts, Azure environments, and the quiet, constant work of keeping enterprise communication running. None of it would have meant as much without the teammates and mentors who shaped it along the way.
